## Information

### Data Sources
- **U-values (thermal transmittance)** and **window g-values (solar energy transmittance)** for non-residential buildings are derived from:
  - Envelope areas and baseline U-values from Hörner et al., 2022, https://github.com/IWUGERMANY/Nichtwohngebaeude-Typologie-DeutschlandIWU Non-Residential Building Typology.
  - Renovation progress data from Hörner et al., 2022, Stand und Dynamik der energetischen Moderni-sierung von Gebäudehülle und haustechnischen Anlagen im Bestand der Nichtwohngebäude.
  - Legal U-value limits for refurbished components from the German Energy Saving Ordinance EnEV 2014 (separate limits for heated vs. not-heated zones) and KfW 55 standard specifications.
  - Heated and non-heated area shares (T_winter threshold 19 °C) from SIA 2024.
  - Reference data for window g-values from Bagherian et al., 2021, https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/veroeffentlichungen/bbsr-online/2021/bbsr-online-37-2021.html.

### Retrofit Levels
Three characteristic retrofit states are distinguished, following the analogy of the TABULA project for residential buildings:

1. **Not retrofitted**
   - U-values represent the original (unrenovated) building envelope.
   - For buildings constructed **before 2010**, IWU mean values are corrected using renovation progress data.
   - For **buildings after 2010**, IWU means are applied directly (new construction, assumed unrenovated).

2. **EnEV 2014 refurbished (partially retrofitted)**
   - Represents compliance with German EnEV 2014 refurbishment thresholds, with **distinct maxima** for **heated** and **not-heated** building zones
   - Because many non-residential buildings include areas with < 19 °C, we compute weighted average U-values using the heated-area share from SIA 2024.
   - If an EnEV 2014 value is higher (worse) than the original, the original value is retained.

3. **KfW 55 refurbished (completely retrofitted)**
   - Represents an ambitious refurbishment level consistent with the KfW 55 efficiency house standard.
   - If a KfW-55 U-value is higher (worse) than the EnEV-2014 value, the lower (better) value is retained.

### Parameter Definitions
| German Description                                      | Key        | Unit    | Explanation in English                                       |
|---------------------------------------------------------|------------|---------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| U-Wert Außenwand                                        | u_aw       | [W/m²K] | U-value of the external wall                                |
| U-Wert Fenster                                          | u_fen      | [W/m²K] | U-value of the windows                                      |
| U-Wert Dach                                             | u_d_opak   | [W/m²K] | U-value of the opaque parts of the roof/top-floor           |
| U-Wert der unteren Gebäudeabschlusses                   | u_ug       | [W/m²K] | U-value of the basement ceiling / lower building closure    |
| g-Wert Fenster                                          | g_gl_fen   | [-]     | Total solar energy transmittance (g-value) of the windows   |

### Envelope Surface Ratios
Surface areas of envelope components are taken from the IWU Non-Residential Building Typology.
As window placement data is unavailable, window areas are assumed to be evenly distributed.

| German Description                                                                 | Key   | Unit   | Explanation in English |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------|--------|-------------------------|
| Verhältnis der Außenwandfläche zur Netto-Raumfläche                                | ow1   | [m²/m²] | Ratio of exterior wall area to net floor area |
| Verhältnis der Fensterfläche zur Netto-Raumfläche                                  | win1  | [m²/m²] | Ratio of window area to net floor area |
| Verhältnis der wärmeübertragenden Fläche des opaken oberen Gebäudeabschlusses zur Netto-Raumfläche | rt1 | [m²/m²] | Ratio of roof/top-floor heat-transmitting area to net floor area |
| Verhältnis der wärmeübertragenden Fläche des unteren Gebäudeabschlusses zur Netto-Raumfläche | gf1 | [m²/m²] | Ratio of basement/floor heat-transmitting area to net floor area |
